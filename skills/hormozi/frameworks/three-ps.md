# Three Ps
slug: three-ps · category: content · aliases: promise proof path

## What it is
A video-opening structure built around promise, proof, and path. After reviewing the first three minutes of his top 35 videos, he identified these three shared elements. Promise and proof give viewers reasons to stay; path establishes what to expect. He believes setting those expectations affects watch time.

## How to apply it
1. Promise: establish what the viewer can expect to gain from watching.
2. Proof: provide evidence that supports the promise and gives the viewer a reason to keep watching.
3. Path: explain what the video will cover so the viewer knows what comes next.

## When to reach for it
- Structuring the opening of a video.
- Reviewing whether an introduction gives viewers a reason to stay and a clear sense of what to expect.
