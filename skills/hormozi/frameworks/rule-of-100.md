# Rule of 100
slug: rule-of-100 · category: leads · aliases: 100 primary actions, 100 days, do more, volume rule

## What it is
Hormozi's concrete method for the "more" in [[more-better-new]]: perform 100 primary advertising actions every day for 100 days straight. It is one of the few outcomes he openly promises: do it and you will get more engaged leads. His logic is that volume beats polish early; doubling inputs with no improvement at all still roughly doubles results, and the biggest jumps usually come from simply advertising more.

## How to apply it
Pick your core four activity and set the daily quota:
- **Warm outreach:** 100 reach-outs a day (email, text, DM, calls).
- **Posting content:** 100 minutes a day creating, publishing at least once daily on one platform, more as you improve.
- **Cold outreach:** 100 reach-outs a day (email, text, DM, calls, flyers). Response is lower with cold, so lean on automation.
- **Paid ads:** 100 minutes a day making ads (direct response, direct mail, seminars, podcast spots), running for 100 consecutive days at the daily budget worked out from your customer goal, aiming for client financed acquisition.

Track it; keep the streak. After volume is maxed and something starts to break, shift attention to testing the weakest step.

## When to reach for it
Early businesses with thin lead flow; owners who "tried" a channel for a couple of weeks; anyone asking for a better tactic before they have done enough reps.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Optimising before doing volume. More comes first because it is usually where the biggest gains are.
- Skipping days. The rule is a streak, not an average.
- Mistaking the pace: people report "100 reach-outs over six weeks, one customer" and conclude it failed. That's about 1/42 of the work; it is 100 a day (p. 362).
- Once 100 a day is routine, graduate to [[open-to-goal]].

## The math
100 actions x 100 days = 10,000 primary actions. Doubling reach-outs, posts or spend with no other change should roughly double leads, because core four inputs and outputs are close to linear.
