# BANT
slug: bant · category: sales · aliases: budget authority need timing

## What it is
A prospect qualification framework Hormozi uses to assess buying readiness. It checks four things: available budget, authority to make the purchase, a relevant need, and timing. These criteria help sales teams prioritize prospects and identify people who are not ready to buy.

## How to apply it
1. Budget: confirm whether the prospect has money available for the purchase.
2. Authority: establish whether the person can make the buying decision.
3. Need: determine whether they have a problem the offer addresses and how strong that need is.
4. Timing: find out when they want to act and whether solving the problem is a current priority.
5. Qualify through the application or setting call before asking for money. Confirm the criteria again on the closing call.
6. For appointments in person, screen by phone before asking the prospect to attend.

## When to reach for it
- Salespeople need to prioritize inbound prospects, direct ad responses, or outbound follow-ups.
- Unqualified leads are reaching appointments in person.
- You want to decide which qualified prospects should receive free help.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Waiting until someone arrives in person to discover that they are unqualified.
- Asking for money before confirming qualification.
- Applying individual qualification to broad live audiences, where it is less suited than direct responses and follow-ups.
