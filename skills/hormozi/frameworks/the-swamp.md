# The Swamp
slug: the-swamp · category: scaling · aliases: the swamp

## What it is
Hormozi's name for the difficult growth stage around $1 million–$3 million in business revenue, when the business needs a key hire but its profit makes that hire difficult to afford. The owner faces two uncomfortable routes: take on enough additional work to fund growth, or commit much of the current profit to someone who may provide the needed capacity.

## How to apply it
1. Compare the cost of the needed hire with current profit to understand the size of the bet.
2. Make the tradeoff explicit: additional founder workload or reduced profit while betting on the hire.
3. Improve cash flow through pricing and packaging first.
4. Put the cash freed up into ads with attribution.
5. Then increase content cadence to build thought leadership.

## When to reach for it
- Revenue is around $1 million–$3 million and hiring would consume much of the profit.
- Growth appears to require the owner to do a second full-time job's work.
- A service business needs more cash flow to support its next stage.

## The math
At $1 million in revenue with 20% profit, the business produces $200,000 in profit. A $200,000 hire could consume all of it. This illustrates why a business can have substantial revenue while still struggling to afford the person it needs.
