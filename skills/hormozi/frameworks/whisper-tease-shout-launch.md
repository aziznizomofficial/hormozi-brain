# Whisper, Tease, Shout (launch sequence)
slug: whisper-tease-shout-launch · category: marketing · aliases: launch method, product launch, affiliate activation launch, pre-launch

## What it is
The launch structure Hormozi uses to activate affiliates, and says works for launching anything. Before purchases open, affiliates (or you) promote either the lead magnet or the core offer to their audience through every Core Four method, then sell to the accumulated engaged leads on launch day. Each phase maps to a part of an ad: whisper = callout, tease = value, shout = call to action. Do all the prep for affiliates so they can plug and play.

## How to apply it
1. **Whisper (curiosity).** Keep the product mysterious and hint that it is a big deal. Short messages; behind-the-scenes of building it. Start as early as you can, even years out, because things that appear to take longer are valued more; show the work. Raise a question in their minds, then say "not yet." For his book he posted draft counts, printouts, framework sketches, and late-night editing clips.
   - Cadence: every 4–6 weeks until 60 days out, then every 2–3 weeks until 30 days out.
2. **Tease (value).** Reveal the product and the launch date, and start showing the value elements with the [[what-who-when-ad-framework]]. For the book: the dream outcome of plentiful leads, with less work and faster, plus many examples.
   - Cadence: weekly until 14 days out, then twice a week until 3 days out.
3. **Shout (CTA).** Give specific actions and pile on launch-only bonuses, scarcity, urgency, and guarantees for early buyers. Maximise exposure.
   - Cadence: at least twice a day from 3 days out; on launch day every few hours until 2 hours out, then every 30 minutes until it opens.

## When to reach for it
Activating a new batch of affiliates; releasing a book, product, event or program; any time you want a spike instead of a trickle.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Making affiliates do the prep work. Hand them everything.
- Revealing too much too early; the whisper phase runs on unanswered questions.

## The math
Only the cadence above. Twice a year his team runs a big launch-style event with affiliates on top of ongoing integration.
