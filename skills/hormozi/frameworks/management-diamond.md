# Management Diamond
slug: management-diamond · category: operations · aliases: performance diamond

## What it is
A diagnostic for understanding why someone is not performing. The Performance Diamond groups the causes into communication, training, motivation, and circumstances. The management questions make those categories concrete: did the person understand the assignment and deadline, know how to do the work, have room to execute without obstacles or competing priorities, and understand why it mattered?

## How to apply it
1. Check communication: establish whether the person knew about the task, understood the assignment, and understood the deadline.
2. Check training: determine whether they knew how to complete it. If the method is missing, learning it becomes the next task.
3. Check circumstances: look for obstacles and conflicting priorities that prevented execution.
4. Check motivation: establish whether the person understood the reason for the work and was willing to do it.
5. Apply the same questions to your own inaction.

## When to reach for it
- An employee misses a deadline or does not complete an assignment.
- A manager attributes a performance problem to unwillingness.
- You are avoiding a task and need to identify what is missing.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Blaming unwillingness before examining more common explanations.
- Treating a missing method as a motivation problem.
