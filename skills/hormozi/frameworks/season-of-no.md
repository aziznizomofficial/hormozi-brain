# Season of No
slug: season-of-no · category: mindset · aliases: season of no

## What it is
A temporary period of declining competing commitments so one major goal receives concentrated attention. The point is to make room for the chosen priority. It can last three months or three years; Hormozi says his longest lasted three years. It is a temporary choice about priorities, rather than a permanent rule for living.

## How to apply it
1. Choose the major goal that deserves your attention during this period.
2. Decline activities and commitments that compete with it.
3. Put the available time into the work the goal requires. His aspiring-entrepreneur example directs spare time outside day jobs toward learning skills, while declining social invitations, alcohol, and leisure distractions.
4. Keep following the plan. He describes saying no as a capacity that gets easier with practice.

## When to reach for it
- Competing commitments keep crowding out a major goal.
- You need time outside an existing job to develop entrepreneurial skills.
- Repeated distractions make it difficult to stay with a chosen plan.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Treating the season as a permanent obligation to reject everything.
- Claiming a priority while continuing to accept activities that displace it.
- Assuming that making the plan is the hardest part; staying with it requires repeated refusals.
