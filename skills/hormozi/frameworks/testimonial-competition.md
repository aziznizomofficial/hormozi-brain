# Testimonial Competition
slug: testimonial-competition · category: marketing · aliases: raw iPhone testimonial contest, ad-performance contest, best testimonial wins

## What it is
A Marketing Machine node where customers compete to make the best raw, phone-shot testimonial. The winner is decided by which submission converts best when run as an ad for 30 days, not by a judge's opinion. Hormozi calls it the node to start with, because it brings the biggest single influx of proof. People tire of competitions, so he limits them to once or twice a year.

## How to apply it
1. **Set an announcement date and a submission deadline.**
2. **Choose a prize big enough to excite people,** for example six months of free product or service.
3. **Give submission instructions:** complete the results template and attach a video, then tag your community manager and send the raw file by email to the proof inbox.
4. **Share a model:** last time's winner, or your best testimonial so far, plus the six-point testimonial script.
5. **Announce it on every internal channel** (email, group chats, community posts) and count down to the deadline.
6. **Personally invite and follow up with the 50 or so customers** you most want a testimonial from. Asking them to record it right now, in five minutes on their phone, works well. The rawer the better.
7. **Close submissions, format each one as an ad and run them.**
8. **Set a reminder 30 days out,** count which ads converted best, and announce the winner publicly.
9. **Space it between your events** so a large batch of new proof arrives every few months.

## When to reach for it
- You need a lot of fresh creative quickly.
- You have an engaged customer base or community but little recorded proof.
- Beginners: his first recommended Marketing Machine node.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Running them too often. The audience gets tired of them.
- Letting people "think about it" instead of asking for the video right away.

## The math
Winner = most conversions over 30 days as an ad. With one competition and two events a year, spaced evenly, you get a big proof influx about every twelve weeks.
