# Delivery Cube (Product Delivery Cheat Codes)
slug: delivery-cube · category: offer · aliases: product delivery cheat codes, one-on-one / small group / one-to-many, DIY DWY DFY, 10x to 1/10th test

## What it is
A set of dials for inventing many ways to deliver the same solution, so you can find versions that feel premium to the buyer but are cheap for you to fulfill. Each dial changes where the offer sits on the value equation: more personal attention and more done-for-you raise perceived likelihood and cut effort, but cost more to deliver.

## How to apply it
Run every solution from your problem list through these dials:
1. Personal attention: one-on-one, small group, or one-to-many.
2. Customer effort: they handle the work themselves, share it with you, or have you handle it entirely.
3. Live medium: in person, phone, email, text, video call, chat.
4. Recorded format: audio, video, or written.
5. Responsiveness: which days and hours, and how fast you reply (minutes, an hour, a day, 24/7).
6. The 10x / 1/10th test: what would you deliver if they paid ten times as much; how would you still make them win at a tenth of the price?

Then trim: keep one-to-many assets with a big value-to-cost gap, and reserve expensive one-on-one work for the few items where it clearly adds value.

## When to reach for it
- You only know one way to deliver and it does not scale.
- You are stuck building the solutions list in [[offer-creation-process]].
- You want a premium tier or a capped "access to you" level (see [[scarcity]]).

## Mistakes he warns about
- Defaulting to the usual delivery format without exploring alternatives.
- Spending high-cost delivery where a cheaper vehicle would create the same value.
- Staying done-for-you forever: Gym Launch's travel-heavy turnarounds capped out at about eight gyms a month; switching to done-with-you let it serve thousands.

## The math
Done-for-you usually commands higher prices than do-it-yourself because it removes effort and raises the buyer's belief it will work. He shifted Gym Launch from flying in and doing everything to teaching owners remotely, accepting maybe a third of the fee per client in exchange for serving hundreds of gyms instead of eight.
