# Levels of Authority (The Branding Cycle)
slug: levels-of-authority · category: marketing · aliases: branding cycle, what we say / what others say / what they experience, brand goes both ways, virtuous and vicious branding cycle

## What it is
Hormozi's model of where brand associations come from and how strong each source is. Associations form fastest through sources people already know and trust. He ranks three layers, weakest to strongest: what **you** say (content, ads, outreach), what **other people** say (affiliates, customers, referrers), and what **they experience themselves** with your product. The cycle runs whether it helps you or hurts you. Advertising shapes the brand in the short term. The product shapes it in the long term, because people spend far more time with the product than with your ads.

## How to apply it
1. **Use your own voice to earn the first sale:** content, ads and outreach make the promise.
2. **Borrow trust from others:** get affiliates, customers and respected figures to vouch for you, since third-party words count for more than yours.
3. **Deliver so the experience confirms the promise.** A strong positive experience brings repeat purchases and referrals, which feed layer two again. That is the virtuous cycle.
4. **Pick endorsers carefully.** The recommender colours how your product is seen (his example: an adult-film star recommending an accounting firm), and your product colours how people see the recommender.
5. **If you charge premium prices, make the product exceptional,** or at least never let it contradict the brand.

## When to reach for it
- Ads win first purchases but repeat rates and referrals are weak.
- Considering affiliates, influencers or spokespeople.
- Word of mouth is working against you: acquisition keeps getting pricier and you keep hunting for a new marketing hack.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Over-promising in ads and under-delivering. That starts the vicious cycle: bad experiences, no repeat buying, customers warning others, and further damage to your reputation with every additional customer.
- Believing reputation can carry a weak product for long. At some point the product has to deliver.
