# Grandfather Frame
slug: grandfather-frame · category: mindset · aliases: grandfather perspective

## What it is
A perspective exercise for appreciating the life already available today. Imagine being 85 and getting to return to your current body, relationships, and circumstances. From that older self's perspective, ordinary experiences become valuable again and present worries can feel less consuming.

## How to apply it
1. Imagine being 85, looking back at your current life.
2. Imagine receiving the chance to inhabit your current body and circumstances again.
3. Notice what that older self would appreciate: physical comfort, health, familiar people, and ordinary experiences.
4. During business stress, use that perspective to appreciate participating in the experience rather than making its value depend entirely on the outcome.

## When to reach for it
- Daily life feels repetitive and its good parts are easy to overlook.
- Business outcomes occupy so much attention that health and relationships fade into the background.
- A current problem feels larger than it would from the perspective of old age.

## Limits
He describes the effect as lasting only minutes. It is a perspective he practices, rather than a permanent state of gratitude.
