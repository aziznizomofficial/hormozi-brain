# CLOSER Framework
slug: closer-framework · category: sales · aliases: closer

## What it is
His six-stage structure for a sales conversation: Clarify intent, Label the problem, Overview past pain, Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, and Reinforce the decision. It moves from understanding why someone came to strengthening their confidence after buying. He uses it to organize sales scripts without claiming it is the ideal method for every situation.

## How to apply it
1. Clarify intent: ask why the prospect came and what they want help with.
2. Label the problem: identify the gap between their current situation and their desired outcome, then confirm the problem they want solved.
3. Overview past pain: review previous attempts to solve the problem and the difficulties they encountered.
4. Sell the vacation: present the desired outcome.
5. Explain away concerns: address the concerns that remain unresolved.
6. Reinforce the decision: reassure the buyer after purchase. Have customer success make a personalized introduction that references their goals and explains the next appointment.

## When to reach for it
- Building a sales script that follows a clear conversational sequence.
- A prospect wanders away from the problem: use questions as checkpoints to bring the conversation back.
- Planning the transition from purchase to customer success so buyers feel confident about their next step.
