# Lead Getters (leverage through other people)
slug: lead-getters · category: leads · aliases: four lead getters, leverage, people who get you leads, Section IV

## What it is
Once you run out of hours doing the core four yourself, you need other people doing it for you. Hormozi calls them lead getters and names four types: **customers** (referrals), **employees**, **agencies**, and **affiliates**. They are not extra advertising methods; they are people who run the same core four on your behalf. Leverage here means results per hour of your time, like a lever that lets a weak person lift a truck.

## How to apply it
- Use the core four to acquire lead getters in the first place (they start as leads and become engaged like anyone else). Then they run the core four for you, so the effort stacks twice.
- Go further: find lead getters who recruit other lead getters. Ideally every lead eventually becomes a lead getter.
- Work up the four scenarios he lays out:
  1. You do all the advertising: high work, low leads, low leverage.
  2. One lead getter does it: low work, same leads, high leverage.
  3. You spend your time recruiting many lead getters: high work, many leads, higher leverage.
  4. You recruit someone who recruits lead getters: low work, climbing leads, highest leverage.
- Start with customers, because you already have them. See [[customer-referrals]], then [[employees-as-lead-getters]], [[agencies]], [[affiliates]].

**Each one's strength (pp. 350–351):** referrals give cheap exponential growth; employees are under your direct control and run the business; agencies teach skills you retain and transfer to employees; established affiliates can operate independently. They usually arrive in that order: first referrals, then a team, then professional help, then (once you can manage people inside the business) partners outside it.

## When to reach for it
Your own capacity caps lead flow; you are already doing more/better/new and cannot add hours.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Treating lead getters as something separate from the core four. You still need the four to get them.
- Stopping at one layer instead of building people who bring more people.
- Expecting instant wins or switching methods after a few losses. Pick a strategy and stick with it; he still expects three to six months to crack a new lead source.

## The math
Scenario 4 is the aim: one recruitment effort, leads that keep rising monthly without your added work. All four lead getters are higher leverage than doing it alone.
