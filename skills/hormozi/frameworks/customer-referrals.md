# Customer Referrals (word of mouth + seven ways to ask)
slug: customer-referrals · category: leads · aliases: word of mouth, referral program, referral growth equation, two-sided referral, bring-a-friend

## What it is
The first of the four lead getters: customers who bring you engaged leads. Hormozi's origin story: Facebook had shut off Gym Launch's ads for two weeks and he hadn't noticed, because word of mouth alone was bringing in about $500k a week. Referrals help on both sides of the ratio: they buy more, buy pricier things, often pay upfront (higher LTGP) and cost nothing to acquire (lower CAC). Unlike the core four, which scale roughly linearly with effort, referrals compound.

Businesses miss out for two reasons: the product isn't good enough (see [[referral-goodwill]]) and they never ask.

## How to apply it
Treat the ask as an offer: show the customer what they get for referring. Seven combinations that worked for him:
1. **One-sided benefit:** pay your average CAC to the referrer or the friend, triggered by a real three-way intro made at the moment of purchase. Always ask about the spouse; offer a household rate.
2. **Two-sided benefit:** split CAC between both people (Dropbox storage, PayPal credit).
3. **Ask at purchase:** on the contract or checkout, ask who they'd like to do this with, and explain that doing it with someone gets better results. One new salesperson broke event records this way; half his sales were referrals.
4. **Negotiation chip:** trade a discount for introductions to a few friends; different terms justify a different price.
5. **Referral events:** 1–4 week promotions (bring-a-friend, spouse challenge, accountability buddy, coach-led teams).
6. **Ongoing program:** mention the benefits of joining with others in all content, outreach and ads.
7. **Unlockable bonuses:** VIP perks, training, status, service levels unlocked by referring plus leaving a testimonial. Cheaper than cash.
Combo he likes: a time-limited gift card worth a third of the program for the customer to hand to a friend, personalised with the friend's name.

## When to reach for it
Always, but first when churn equals or beats new referrals.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Asking without building goodwill: customers only refer when benefit to them outweighs the risk to their friendship.
- Waiting to ask; ask right when they buy.

## The math
- Growth equation: referrals in minus churn out. Above churn, you grow with no ads; equal, you need ads to grow; below, you advertise just to stand still.
- A 4:1 LTGP:CAC where each customer brings two more becomes 12:1.
- Examples: $500 program, $200 CAC, $100 cash to referrer plus $100 off for the friend, up to three friends. Dropbox grew 39x in fifteen months; PayPal hit a million users in two years.
