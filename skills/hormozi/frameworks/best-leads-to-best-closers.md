# Best Leads to Best Closers (Lead Scoring and Routing)
slug: best-leads-to-best-closers · category: sales · aliases: lead scoring, red-yellow-green, qualify the leads, lead routing, cancel bad appointments

## What it is
A personalization tactic that sends a sales team's scarce time where it pays most. You score each lead on how likely they are to buy, clear hopeless ones off the calendar, and route the highest-scoring leads to the reps who close best. Hormozi learned it from two businesses. One was a weight-loss company that deliberately cancelled sales calls with applicants who were a bad fit and made unusually high profits. The other was the top rep at a large US timeshare company. That rep persuaded the CEO to give top reps the best leads, and in Hormozi's telling the company then grew from about $200M to $1B in yearly sales within five years.

## How to apply it
1. **Profile your best customers:** what they did and who they are (demographics).
2. **Ask those questions up front** in opt-ins, applications and the calls before the sale.
3. **Score every lead** on a 1–5 scale or red/yellow/green.
4. **Route** the top scores to your top closers. Remove clear misfits from the calendar and offer them free help, referrals or a downsell instead.
5. **Use the scores to segment** follow-up messages and matched proof.
6. **Do not throw yellows away.** In his execution story, a hungry junior rep took everyone's "yellow" leads on evenings and weekends and became top seller, which gave rise to the team saying "yellows are the new gold."

## When to reach for it
- Top reps spend much of their time on leads who cannot buy (the timeshare rep complained about sub-500 credit scores).
- Close rates vary hugely by lead source or lead quality.
- The calendar is full but revenue is flat.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Spreading premium leads evenly or randomly, so junior reps burn the best opportunities.
- Treating every booked appointment as sacred. Cancelling poor fits frees time for buyers.
- Writing off less-qualified leads completely. They are still sales and free practice for newer reps.

## The math
The timeshare rep reportedly grew his office's output 5x in the first year of the pilot. No formula is given.
