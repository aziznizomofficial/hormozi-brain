# Niche Down (Commit to the Niche)
slug: niche-down · category: marketing · aliases: niche slap, riches are in the niches, niche pricing, when to broaden

## What it is
Serving one narrowly defined buyer lets you charge many times more for essentially the same product, because the messaging and application feel made for them. It also means you must commit: hopping between niches resets your learning every time. His term "niche slap" is the reminder he gives owners who keep switching markets.

## How to apply it
1. Pick one avatar inside a normal-or-better market ([[starving-crowd]]). Gym Launch accepted only microgym owners focused on weight loss who had about 100 members, had signed a lease, and employed at least one person.
2. Stack specificity: who they are, what problem, and ideally a distinctive method that removes their biggest fear.
3. Rewrite the product's framing, examples and outcomes for that avatar; tie the price to what the result is worth to them.
4. Stay long enough to fail repeatedly and iterate offers. Try many offers before judging the market.
5. Broaden only as you near the total addressable market's ceiling: target higher-end buyers, lower-end buyers, or a neighbouring market.

## When to reach for it
- Selling to "small business owners" or "anyone who pays".
- Low prices because your product looks generic.
- You are thinking of switching niches after one or two failed offers.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Believing a stalled business at $1-3M/yr must expand to new markets; usually it just needs to get better. Many companies passed $30M/yr in one niche.
- Switching dentists to chiropractors (both fine markets) instead of fixing the offer. Pick one; you cannot serve two masters.
- Taking a failed offer personally; the offer failed, not you.

## The math
Dan Kennedy's example as retold: a generic time-management course at about $19; for sales professionals about $99; for B2B outbound reps about $499 (a 25x jump); for outbound reps selling power tools and gardening gear, $1,000-2,000. Likewise a generic weight-loss program at $19 versus one for shift nurses at $1,997. Guideline: below about $10M/yr, narrowing usually earns more.
