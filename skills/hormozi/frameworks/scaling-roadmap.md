# Scaling Roadmap
slug: scaling-roadmap · category: scaling · aliases: 100m scaling roadmap

## What it is
A guide that groups businesses into generalized stages to help identify what needs to improve for growth. Hormozi emphasizes that the actual constraint matters more than the revenue bucket: what is stopping the company from carrying out its growth activity? One specified stage is level six, covering businesses with 20–49 employees. For inconsistent core-product sales at that stage, he recommends training, individual coaching, and regular team accountability to performance targets.

## How to apply it
1. Identify what prevents the business from doing the activity that would produce growth. Use its stage as context for that diagnosis.
2. For a level six business with inconsistent core-product sales, strengthen training, coach individuals, and regularly hold the team accountable to performance targets.
3. Prioritize improvements with substantial impact and little work, accounting for the resources needed to implement them.
4. Schedule larger projects later unless other improvements depend on completing them first.
5. Reassess the constraint as the business grows. Solving insufficient demand can expose limited capacity, and expanding capacity can expose insufficient demand.

## When to reach for it
- You need to decide what is preventing further growth.
- A business with 20–49 employees has inconsistent sales of its core product.
- Several improvements compete for limited implementation resources.
- A previous fix has exposed a different bottleneck.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Letting a generalized revenue bucket outweigh the actual constraint.
- Prioritizing changes without considering their impact, implementation effort, or dependencies.
- Continuing to address demand when capacity has become the constraint, or capacity when demand has become the constraint.
