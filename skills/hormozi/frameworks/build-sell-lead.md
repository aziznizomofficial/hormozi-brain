# Build, Sell, Lead
slug: build-sell-lead · category: scaling · aliases: sell build lead

## What it is
His grouping of three core business skills: building, selling, and leading. Building concerns the product. Selling includes both marketing and converting prospects into customers. Leading means getting people to carry out the desired actions at scale.

## How to apply it
1. Build: develop the product the business provides.
2. Sell: cover both making people aware of the offering and converting that attention into purchases.
3. Lead: develop the ability to get other people to carry out the work at scale.
