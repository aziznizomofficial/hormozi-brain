# Logic, Evidence, Utility
slug: logic-evidence-utility · category: mindset · aliases: logic evidence utility

## What it is
A three-part check for evaluating assertions and complex challenges. Clarify what the assertion means, examine the support for it, and determine its practical relevance. Together, these checks address definition, proof, and impact.

## How to apply it
1. Clarify the meaning: define the words and state the assertion precisely enough to examine it.
2. Examine the support: identify the evidence and reasoning behind the assertion.
3. Check its utility: determine why it matters and what practical difference it makes.
4. Write the reasoning in complete sentences to expose logical gaps that vague thinking can conceal.

## When to reach for it
- A belief or assertion is too vague to evaluate clearly.
- A complex challenge needs a systematic examination of meaning, evidence, and practical impact.
- An argument feels clear in your head but becomes difficult to explain in writing.
