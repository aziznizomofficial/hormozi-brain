# Annual Pricing Options (Big Head, Long Tail)
slug: annual-pricing-options · category: pricing · aliases: annual billing, prepay option, buy 10 months get 2 free, big head long tail, one-time fee plus small monthly, founder rates

## What it is
Customers who pay for a longer stay tend to stay longer. Adding an annual or prepaid option locks some customers in for a full year, which lengthens average lifetime and brings cash in up front. Hormozi's variant is the **big head, long tail**: charge a large one-time fee for the thing with one-time value (education, setup, implementation), then a small recurring fee for the ongoing value (community, access). Step 6 of his Churn Checklist.

## How to apply it
1. **Checkout or website sales:** keep monthly and add an annual option. At "buy 10 months, get 2 free," about 10–20% choose annual. A steeper annual discount pulls in more.
2. **Phone or webinar sales:** consider making annual the only option. Sales drop, but churn drops too, and total revenue can come out higher.
3. **Set the annual price above what the average customer pays now.** If people stay 6 months at $1,000, the annual price must exceed $6,000. If they already stay 12+ months, use the 10-for-12 structure (about 16% off).
4. **Big head, long tail:** price the up-front piece on its one-time value and the recurring piece on the ongoing value. Sell both together as one purchase, because splitting them loses the price anchor that supports retention.
5. **Founder rates:** a deep discount (around 50%, if margins allow) for early or owner-level customers encourages both buying and staying.
6. Present the options with the most cash up front first.

## When to reach for it
Monthly churn is high, cash flow is tight, the product has a large one-time component, or you're pricing a community or membership.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Pricing the annual plan below what an average month-to-month customer already pays you.
- Selling the recurring fee as a separate later sale from the big up-front fee.
- Making recurring billing out of something with only one-time value.

## The math
- Big head, long tail: $6,800 up front plus $199/month. Over about 30 months the $199 tail lifts LTV from $6,800 to about $12,800 (p. 26).
- Agency example with the same ad spend: charging a $3,997 one-time fee instead of $1,000 recurring, with a better offer, turned $5,000 of cash into $112,000 (22.4x) (p. 27).
- ProfitWell: monthly billing about 10.7% monthly churn, quarterly 5%, annual 2% (Money Models p. 118).
