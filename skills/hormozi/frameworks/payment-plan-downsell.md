# Payment Plan Downsell
slug: payment-plan-downsell · category: sales · aliases: payment plans, seesaw downsell, half now half later, reward pay-in-full, when-do-you-get-paid close

## What it is
A downsell that keeps the product and the total price the same and changes only **when** the customer pays. A too-expensive objection usually means too expensive today, so spreading payments wins buyers the way a discount would while you still collect full price. It's a gamble: it wins when extra buyers finish paying, and loses on early cancellations, worst of all from people who would have paid in full.

## How to apply it
Step down in order and stop the moment they buy:
1. **Reward paying in full rather than punishing paying over time.** Quote the price with interest built in, then offer a discount for prepaying.
2. Offer **third-party financing**, then a **credit card** (ask whether they'd rather set their own terms), then **layaway**.
3. **Half now, half on their next payday.** If that's too much, ask for the most they can put down today.
4. **Check desire.** Ask them to rate how much they want it from 1 to 10. At 8 or above, keep going. At 7 or below, switch to a feature downsell.
5. **Three payments**: one third now, the rest on paydays or monthly.
6. **Even payments** across the whole term (e.g., weekly for 16 weeks).
7. **Free trial**, done as a trial with penalty.

**Seesaw version** (fewer steps, suits junior reps): ask whether they'd prefer giant or tiny monthly payments, frame prepaying as the way to avoid payments altogether, then let a bigger down payment shrink the monthly amount.

## When to reach for it
High-ticket sales where price objections come up, new closers, and any business with no answer when someone says they can't afford it.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Dropping the price or haggling instead. That trades away trust, and customers compare notes.
- Watch the mix. Overall closes should rise **while the share paying in full stays the same.** If full payments fall, you're cannibalizing.
- Give people a reason to pay faster. Periodically re-offer the pay-in-full discount on any remaining balance, and pay reps a bonus for closing it.

## The math
- Schedule charges on paydays and retry declined cards several times that day. This recovers about a third of declines (p. 118).
- ProfitWell data: monthly billing ran about 10.7% monthly churn, quarterly 5%, annual 2%. Present the fewest, largest payments first (p. 118).
