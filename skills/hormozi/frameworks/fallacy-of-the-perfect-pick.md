# Fallacy of the Perfect Pick
slug: fallacy-of-the-perfect-pick · category: mindset · aliases: fallacy of the perfect pick

## What it is
The mistaken expectation that one ideal opportunity will deliver success without meaningful costs or tradeoffs. Searching for that option can turn into analysis paralysis. Hormozi emphasizes choosing, gaining practical experience, and developing capability through attempts instead of expecting the initial selection to do the work.

## How to apply it
1. Identify the costs and problems that come with each available path.
2. Name what each pursuit would displace from your already occupied life.
3. When you lack experience, test possibilities so you gain a basis for judging what fits.
4. Choose a workable path without requiring it to be free of drawbacks.
5. Once you choose, commit enough attention to it that divided focus does not keep returning.

## When to reach for it
- You keep postponing a decision because every option has a downside.
- You expect the right business opportunity to remove the need to develop capability.
- You are trying to judge a pursuit without practical experience of it.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Searching for a path with no costs.
- Treating the opportunity itself as the main explanation for future success.
- Demanding certainty before gaining the experience needed to judge your options.
- Making a choice without committing to it afterward.
