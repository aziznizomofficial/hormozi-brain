# Award and Bonus-Unlock Testimonials
slug: award-and-bonus-unlock-testimonials · category: marketing · aliases: bonus unlocks, award testimonials, milestone awards, proof@ inbox, four proofs from one award, unlockables

## What it is
A Marketing Machine node where customers earn something they want by producing proof for you. Hormozi says he felt silly when he realised it. He had been handing out awards at events and getting nothing back, when he could have required a testimonial to claim them. There are two versions: a **bonus unlock** (a paid extra that customers can get free by submitting a testimonial) and an **award** (a status symbol for hitting an aspirational milestone, claimed with proof). A single award can produce four separate pieces of proof.

## How to apply it
**Bonus unlock**
1. Create something people want (training, a small project, access for a single occasion, or a tier that confers status), assign it an actual selling price, and sell it.
2. Record yourself pitching it where every customer will see it, then offer it free to anyone who sends a testimonial video.
3. Give exact instructions: under 5 minutes, covering the six-point script, emailed to a proof inbox (for example, proof@yourco.com), with your best example linked. Also ask them to post it in the community with their numbers, tag the community manager and use scrape-friendly keywords, which gets you a written version too.

**Award**
1. Set an aspirational milestone if you do not already have one. His gym owners aimed for a $1M-a-year gym.
2. Make a trophy tied to your community's identity that people will actually crave, and spend real money on it. Gym Launch used a sword and shield for its "Gym Lords."
3. Announce it on video: what they get, plus public recognition at the next event.
4. To claim it: a community post showing their numbers in your testimonial template (proof 1), a video emailed to the proof inbox (proof 2), an unboxing video of the engraved award you mail them (proof 3), and footage of you presenting a generic copy on stage (proof 4).

## When to reach for it
- Customers are getting results but rarely volunteer testimonials.
- You want a steady weekly flow of video proof.
- Your community needs a status goal to chase.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Giving recognition away without asking for proof in return.
- A boring or cheap award. If people do not crave it, they will not bother.

## The math
One award = four pieces of proof per winner.
