# Continuity Bonus Offer
slug: continuity-bonus-offer · category: money-model · aliases: bonus continuity, gift-wrapping continuity, sign-up-today bonus, sliding scale (standalone vs membership)

## What it is
Give a valuable bonus to anyone who joins your recurring offer today. The bonus is usually worth more than the first payment. Nobody wants to commit to paying every month for something untried, so you advertise the free thing and explain the membership once they're interested. A gym that made its challenge free for anyone joining as a member, plus member-only perks, roughly tripled membership sales (17 → 40 per 100) and still sold some challenges for cash up front.

## How to apply it
1. Choose bonuses you **already have or already do**: past newsletters, onboarding, a defined program, setup. Keep them close to the core offer so you attract the right buyers.
2. Match bonuses the way you'd match upsells: more of the same, something complementary, or an upgrade (bronze buyers get gold, while supplies last). Try pairing digital products with physical bonuses, and physical products with digital bonuses. Give status titles for tenure (silver, gold, diamond).
3. Sell the bonus first and state its real value. Then ask whether they'd like to know how to get it free: join today. Stack more bonuses, each with its dollar value.
4. Decide whether the bonus is **exclusive to members** (pushes everyone into continuity) or also sold on its own at a higher one-time price (more cash up front).
5. Once they join, upsell a prepaid block of time at a discount.
6. To get commitments, give the bonus only for 3-, 6- or 12-month terms. You'll get fewer sales but more committed customers.

## When to reach for it
The front end sells but customers won't convert to recurring. You're in a low-cash-flow industry. A standalone membership costs too much to advertise.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Advertising "join my membership" instead of the free valuable thing.
- Inflated or made-up bonus values. They don't anchor and they erode trust.
- Unrelated bonuses (a free t-shirt to sell tech services) attract the wrong people.

## The math
Price the bonus-only one-time option relative to the monthly membership. Rough share choosing continuity: 1.33x → 50%, 1.66x → 60%, 2x → 70%, 2.33x → 80%, 2.66x → 90% (p. 150). In other words, half of buyers will pay about 33% more to avoid continuity (p. 151). Prepayment for five months with a sixth month free raises 30-day profit by 50% if just 1 in 8 members accepts (p. 151).
