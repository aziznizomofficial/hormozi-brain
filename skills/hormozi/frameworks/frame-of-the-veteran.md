# Frame of the Veteran
slug: frame-of-the-veteran · category: mindset · aliases: veteran frame

## What it is
A perspective exercise Hormozi credits to Dr. Cashy. Imagine experiencing an upsetting event 1,000 times and consider how familiar it would feel by the last repetition. If repeated exposure would make it ordinary, try adopting that calmer perspective now. He uses the exercise to reduce the intensity of his reaction to everyday frustrations.

## How to apply it
1. Identify the event that is upsetting you.
2. Imagine it recurring 1,000 times.
3. Consider how strongly you would react after that much familiarity.
4. If the event would no longer feel exceptional, approach the current occurrence with that anticipated familiarity.

He describes first using the exercise after his maid shrank a flannel shirt he liked. Imagining that clothes always behaved this way helped him stop treating the incident as unusually upsetting, and he reports that his annoyance ended.

## When to reach for it
- A recurring hassle feels like a new crisis each time.
- An everyday disappointment provokes a stronger reaction than imagined familiarity would.
- You want to practice responding to an unpleasant event as something expected and ordinary.
