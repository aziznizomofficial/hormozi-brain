# Guarantees (Risk Reversal)
slug: guarantees · category: offer · aliases: risk reversal, unconditional, conditional, anti-guarantee, implied guarantee, performance pricing, service guarantee, better than money back

## What it is
Risk is the biggest objection to any purchase, so reversing it is the fastest way to lift conversion (he cites 2-4x swings from guarantee quality alone). A guarantee gets its teeth from a conditional promise: if you do not get X by Y, we will do Z. Without the "or what," it is just a claim.

## How to apply it
Four types:
1. Unconditional: pay, try, refund if unhappy. Strongest; suits lower-ticket B2C.
2. Conditional: terms tied to the actions that produce success, ideally "better than money back." Suits higher-ticket and B2B.
3. Anti-guarantee: all sales final, with a convincing reason (what you reveal cannot be unseen, or you only want committed clients).
4. Implied: performance deals (rev share, profit share, per-show or per-result fees, ratchets, triggers); they need transparent measurement and a reliable way to collect. Can pair with a minimum fee.

Menu of conditional variants: outsized refund (2-3x back), keep serving free until the result, extra service period, credit toward other services, personal one-on-one help, refund of ancillary costs (travel, ad spend, their hourly wage), release from contract, no second bill until the first result, you cover costs until the first result.

Build your own: list what the buyer fears most and reverse it. Stack guarantees (e.g., 30-day unconditional plus a 90-day conditional). Give it a vivid name.

## When to reach for it
- Prospects agree it would work "for others" but fear it will not work for them.
- Sales stall at the close.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Using a guarantee to cover a weak product or sales team; refunds will explode.
- Unconditional refunds on high-cost fulfillment; you eat both the refund and the cost.
- Attracting buyers who only bought for the guarantee and won't do the work.
- Deciding emotionally instead of by net sales.

## The math
100 sales with 5% refunds = 95 net; a stronger guarantee giving 130 sales at 10% refunds = 117 net, 1.23x. His weight-loss satisfaction guarantee was claimed by two people out of about 4,000 sales. A $25,000 store-buyback guarantee on a $2,997 course reportedly added about $3M in sales with 10 payouts.
