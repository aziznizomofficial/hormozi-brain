# Appointment Availability
slug: appointment-availability · category: leads · aliases: availability pillar, more time slots, sell on weekends, 15-minute start times, self-scheduling

## What it is
The first and strongest pillar of lead nurture. In Hormozi's ALAN data, the total number of bookable slots (days × hours × start times per hour) tracked throughput more closely than anything else, speed included. If a lead cannot find a time that suits them, they either never book, or they book a bad time and then skip it. A bad-time booking also takes a slot away from someone who would have come and bought.

## How to apply it
1. **Take appointments seven days a week.** Rent and payroll run every day, so sales can too. Going from five to seven days adds about 40% more bookable time.
2. **Extend the hours** to when buyers are free, not when they are at work. For nationwide US sales his team found 6am–6pm Pacific covered most sales. The checklist target is 9am–9pm Eastern.
3. **Offer more start times:** four per hour (on the quarter hour) instead of one or two, while keeping the same appointment length. If you split qualifying and closing calls, a short 15-minute qualifying call makes this easy.
4. **Give three ways to book:** you call them, they call you, and online self-scheduling.
5. **Tune the scheduler page:** a header that confirms they are in the right place plus a clear headline and subheadline, time slots visible as soon as the page loads on any device, the fewest possible steps, and no asking again for details they already gave.
6. **Too many junk bookings?** Add friction on purpose: a video or sales letter before the calendar, the price on the page, or a delayed reveal of the scheduler.

## When to reach for it
- Leads say no time suits them, or book far out.
- Calendars only open during office hours on weekdays.
- Show rates are low even though interest is high.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Claiming you cannot be open more. What people usually mean is that it does not feel worth it yet. Ask what it would really cost, and what staying closed burns.
- Worrying about awkward gaps between appointments. The next pillar (speed) fills those gaps with lead calls.

## The math
Seven days vs five is about 40% more capacity to take money. He reports that these tactics commonly produce 20–40% more appointments that actually show, and sometimes over 200%.
