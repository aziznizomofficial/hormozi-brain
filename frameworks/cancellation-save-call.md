# Cancellation Save Call (Exit Interview)
slug: cancellation-save-call · category: retention · aliases: exit interview, cancellation call, cancellation video, save with a redo, save with an upsell

## What it is
A required conversation before anyone cancels. Some cancelling customers just need to vent, some have a fixable problem, and some were sold the wrong tier. Hormozi reports saving about half of the people who actually get on a cancellation call. Even the ones who leave give you the most honest feedback you'll get. It is one of the Five Horsemen and step 7 of the Churn Checklist.

## How to apply it
1. **Set the expectation at onboarding:** cancelling requires a short call. Then treat every cancellation notice as the trigger to book it.
2. **Give them a reason to show up.** Offer to waive the cancellation fee if they come in and tell you what you could do better.
3. **Let them vent, and be more upset about it than they are.** Only one person can be angry at a time. Agree they're right, ask for a chance to make it right, and confirm what a fix would look like so you have a clear plan.
4. **Save it one of two ways:** a **redo** (another attempt, done properly) or an **upsell** (they needed more help, so credit what they've paid toward the higher tier and own the mistake of under-selling them).
5. **Resell the value:** tie staying to their original goals and remind them what they'd lose (data, work, status, founder pricing, custom URLs).
6. **Low-ticket, high-volume businesses:** use a cancellation video that reminds people why they started and what they'd give up.
7. Make the cancel path easy to find. Hidden cancellations turn into public complaints and give you no chance to save them.

## When to reach for it
Any recurring or membership business, especially when churn is rising, and whenever someone asks for a refund (try a rollover first).

## Mistakes he warns about
- Relying on email or text alone. Saves are much likelier on a live call.
- Arguing or blaming the customer.
- Making cancellation hard to find, which pushes complaints outside the business.

## The math
- About half of call attendees are saved. If half of cancelling customers attend, churn drops about 25%, roughly a 33% LTV lift (Retention p. 7).
- Offering to waive the fee for an exit interview saves about a third of those who agree (Money Models p. 159).
