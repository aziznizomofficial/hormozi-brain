# Hook, Retain, Reward (The Content Unit)
slug: hook-retain-reward · category: content · aliases: content unit, topics-headlines-format, lists steps stories, value per second

## What it is
Hormozi's building block for any content that grows an audience. For content to pay off, a person needs a reason to start, must stay long enough, and must get that reason satisfied. So every piece must hook, retain, and reward attention. The smallest chunk that does all three (a meme, a sentence, a jingle) is a content unit. Longer content is just units chained together; long form is harder only because you must deliver more good units in a row.

## How to apply it
Hook, measured by the share of people who start consuming:
- Topic: personal experience differentiates. Five sources: far past (lessons, "the story without the scar"), recent past (meetings, client stories, case studies), present (capture ideas the moment they occur, post them publicly to test), trending, and manufactured (go do something interesting, then show it).
- Headline: include at least two news-style components: recency, relevance, celebrity, proximity, conflict, unusualness, ongoing story.
- Format: copy the look of the best content your target audience already consumes on that platform.
Retain, mainly with curiosity: plant questions using lists (flexible), steps (ordered, explicit payoff) and stories (what happens next). Mix and nest them.
Reward: fully deliver what the hook promised, to the right audience. The judge is the audience; if it is not growing, the content is not good enough yet.

## When to reach for it
- Planning, scripting or reviewing any post, video, podcast or email.
- Diagnosing low views (hook), low watch time (retain) or low shares/follows (reward).

## Mistakes he warns about
- Blaming length. He says content is never too long, only too boring.
- Hooks that over-promise, or delivering a partial list or stale tips.
- Starting with long form before mastering short units, like a comic who needs years before earning an hour set.

## The math
Quality is value per second of consumption. Hook effectiveness is the percentage who start.
