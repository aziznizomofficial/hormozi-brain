# Depth-Then-Width vs Width-Then-Depth
slug: depth-vs-width · category: scaling · aliases: scaling content across platforms, omnipresence, platform expansion

## What it is
Hormozi's two ways to scale a warm audience across platforms once content is working. Both are valid; they trade compounding efficiency against reach and platform risk.

## How to apply it
Depth then width:
1. Post on one relevant platform, then post regularly.
2. Push quality and quantity to the max there. His rough ceilings: short form up to around ten posts a day per platform; long form up to about five days a week.
3. Add the next platform while holding the first steady; repeat until all relevant platforms are maxed.

Width then depth:
1. Post on one platform, then regularly.
2. Add the next platform before maximising the first, keeping each one going.
3. Once you are on every relevant platform, ramp volume everywhere at once, repurposing the same piece across similar formats.

## When to reach for it
- Depth first: limited people and money; you want to ride compounding on one channel.
- Width first: you already have a sizable business and a team, and want broad reach quickly.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Depth first leaves you dependent on one channel; platforms change rules and ban accounts without warning.
- Width first takes more labour and attention; the common failure is thin, bad content spread everywhere.
- Waiting. His line: the best time to start was long ago, the second best is today.

## The math
No fixed numbers beyond the posting-frequency ceilings above. The logic: audiences compound with volume, so depth maximises return per unit of effort, while width multiplies distribution of each piece.
