# Proof Scraping (Social, Chat, Community, Reviews)
slug: proof-scraping · category: marketing · aliases: social media scrape, chat scrape, community wins, review scrape, screenshot ads, wins of the week

## What it is
Marketing Machine nodes that collect proof customers are already producing in the places you talk to them, then turn it into ads. They are the easiest to start because the material already exists and needs little editing. Hormozi scrapes four sources: social media tags, support and account-rep chats, posts in communities you run, and review sites.

## How to apply it
1. **Social media:** give customers a hashtag or saying to post with and a profile to tag (for example, #gymlaunch, #poweredbyprestige). Set reminders to save tagged posts, especially stories that vanish within 24 hours. File them with searchable names, keep a separate folder of standout performers, and pair the best images and videos with ad copy that already works. Your own best posts count too. Strong organic results tend to predict strong ad and outbound results.
2. **Support/rep chats:** list the words that signal a win (sales, profit, revenue, closed, leads, win, and so on). Reps search their messages for them on a schedule, screenshot new wins and drop them into a shared folder with optional subfolders. Managers praise whoever submits. The optional extra is a weekly gift card for anyone whose screenshot becomes a winning ad.
3. **Community wins:** create a "wins" post category and folder. Prompt "wins of the week" every Friday, screenshot them on Monday using the same keywords, and screen-record a scroll through the comments to make another ad.
4. **Reviews:** list every review site you appear on (Google, Yelp, Facebook, Trustpilot, Amazon, Glassdoor), check each one newest-first on a schedule, screenshot the good ones, and reward whoever collects them.
5. **Use them:** screenshots, scroll recordings of many replies, and search-then-scroll recordings become faceless ads.

## When to reach for it
- Early on, with few resources. Chat, community, review and social scrapes are items 2–5 in his rollout order.
- You have plenty of happy customers but thin ad creative.
- The founder hates photoshoots and recording.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Filling folders and never running the material as ads.
- Not rewarding the team for collecting. Without kudos or incentives they stop.
