# The Advertising Roadmap (zero to $100M)
slug: advertising-roadmap-seven-levels · category: scaling · aliases: seven levels of advertisers, $100M leads machine, putting it all together

## What it is
The staged path Acquisition.com uses to take portfolio companies from a few million a year toward $100M+. Each level has one primary action; use it to locate yourself and see what comes next.

## How to apply it
1. **Friends know.** One offer, one avatar, one platform. Primary action: warm outreach.
2. **Everyone you know knows, consistently.** You know the exact inputs per engaged lead and have maxed your personal capacity. Action: as much warm outreach and content as you can sustain. (He started with paid ads and a case-study lead magnet but wishes he had started with content.)
3. **Employees advertise for you.** You've hit your own limit, not the platform's. Action: hire people to advertise profitably (his first hires were a videographer and a media buyer).
4. **Product earns consistent referrals.** Aim for 25%+ of customers from referrals. He updated the product from customer feedback every two weeks and launched a big-incentive referral program. Action: fix product until referrals are steady, then scale ads with a bigger team.
5. **More places, more ways, more people.** New audiences on your best platform, then every placement and media type, then another platform, lead getter, or core four activity. Action: profitable on at least two methods across several platforms. (He aimed ads at prospective affiliates, which seeded his affiliate programs.)
6. **Hire killers.** Experienced executives who build departments per method or platform without you; hire proven specialists, not potential, and give them strong incentives. He capped here with his early companies; widening ownership at Acquisition.com took the portfolio past $100M, then $200M.
7. Left open; he says he'll add it after crossing a billion.

**The $100M machine:** a media team shipping content everywhere; regular offers to the warm audience; launches profitable immediately; paid-ad teams on several platforms; a cold outreach team; an affiliate manager launching and integrating partners; recruiters bringing in lead getters; a third of customers referring; executives running growth without you.

## When to reach for it
Deciding the next hire or channel; diagnosing why growth stalled at a given size.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Level 4 is where most people slip: the product quality drops and never recovers.
- Expecting it to look clean. Real business is messy; you only learn what works by trying many things for long enough.

## The math
Five to ten years for owners who know what to do; he and his wife took over ten years to reach their first $100M in net worth. Longer goals need longer horizons.
