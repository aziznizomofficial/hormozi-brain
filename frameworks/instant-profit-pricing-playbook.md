# Instant Profit Pricing Playbook (10 Pricing Plays)
slug: instant-profit-pricing-playbook · category: pricing · aliases: pricing easter eggs, just raise it, round up, sales tax pass-through, annual renewal fee, rate protection fee

## What it is
Ten small pricing changes that barely touch conversion or operations yet drop almost entirely to profit; on thin margins, small price lifts become large profit lifts. One or two that fit is enough.

## How to apply it
The ten plays and his estimated revenue lift:
1. Monthly to 28-day billing, 8.3% ([[billing-frequency]])
2. Card processing fee plus backup payment method, 3-4% ([[processing-fee-second-payment]])
3. Sales tax passed through, 0-10%
4. Annual inflation-linked increase, 3-10% ([[annual-price-increases]])
5. Longer billing options, 10-15% ([[billing-frequency]])
6. Round up, 1-3%
7. Annual renewal fee on top of monthly, 10%
8. Automatic continuity, 10% ([[automatic-continuity]])
9. Ultra-high-ticket option, 10-15% ([[price-anchor]])
10. Priced guarantee or warranty, 5-20% ([[warranty-upsell]])

Plays without their own card:
- Sales tax: if you are legally charged it, add it as a separate line on the invoice after price is agreed, matter-of-factly; if they object, side with them against the tax. You cannot charge tax you do not owe.
- Round up: turn price endings of 7 into 9 and add .99; skip this for luxury goods, which should end in round numbers.
- Annual renewal fee: keep the advertised monthly price and add a yearly fee of 1-3x the monthly rate, justified as rate protection or prepaying the cancellation fee. Having both a setup fee and a renewal fee lets you waive one as a closing incentive.

## When to reach for it
- Thin net margins (most small businesses run 7-10%) and you need more profit without changing product or sales process.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Assuming tiny percentages don't matter.
- Absorbing sales tax yourself; at 20% margins a 5% tax gives away a quarter of profit.

## The math
- A restaurant adding a 4% processing fee: $1,000,000 revenue at 9% margin goes from $90,000 to $130,000 profit (+44%).
- Combined estimated lift 26.8-63.8%; +26.8% on a $1M, 9%-margin business takes profit from $90,000 to $358,000.
- Round up: $47 to $49.99 per week adds about $155 per client per year (+6.4%).
- Renewal fee: $39/mo plus $99/yr lifts $468 to $567 per year, an effective $47/mo (+20%); a fee of 1x monthly adds 8.3%, 3x adds 24.9%.
