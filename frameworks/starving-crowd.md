# Starving Crowd (Market Selection)
slug: starving-crowd · category: marketing · aliases: picking the right market, four market indicators, market > offer > persuasion, health wealth relationships

## What it is
Demand beats skill. If a crowd desperately wants something, even a mediocre offer from a poor salesperson sells; in a dying market, nothing works. He treats market choice as the first lever, above the offer and above persuasion. You are not trying to create demand, only to channel demand that already exists.

## How to apply it
Score the market on four indicators:
1. Massive pain: they need it, not just want it. The depth of pain caps the price you can charge.
2. Purchasing power: they can afford your price (his friend's resume service failed because the buyers were unemployed).
3. Easy to target: they gather somewhere you can reach (associations, lists, groups, channels).
4. Growing: a tailwind. His friend's software for newspapers had pain, money and targeting, but the market shrank 25% a year.

Then go narrow inside one of the evergreen buckets (health, wealth, relationships) toward a subgroup that is in pain, can pay, is findable and is growing, e.g. relationship coaching for older singles rather than college students.

Use the priority order: market, then offer strength, then persuasion. A great higher lever overrides a bad lower one; a bad one stops everything unless a higher lever is great. Most readers are in a normal market, so a Grand Slam Offer is their lever.

## When to reach for it
- Everything has been tried (offer, sales, team) and the business still declines.
- Buyers love the product but cannot pay.
- You cannot figure out where to show your ads.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Romanticizing an audience instead of serving the ones who can pay.
- Blaming the market too early; most "bad markets" are really bad offers (see [[niche-down]]).
- Stubbornly fighting a shrinking market because entrepreneurs hate quitting.

## The math
"Normal" market = growing about as fast as the overall economy with ordinary unmet needs. Priority: Starving Crowd > Offer Strength > Persuasion Skills.
