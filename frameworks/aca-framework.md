# A-C-A Conversation Framework
slug: aca-framework · category: sales · aliases: acknowledge-compliment-ask, ACA

## What it is
A simple three-beat pattern Hormozi gives for replying to people in outreach so conversations feel human instead of salesy. Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask. It works for talking to anyone, and it lets you steer a friendly chat toward the topic your offer serves.

## How to apply it
1. Acknowledge: repeat back what they told you in your own words, which proves you listened.
2. Compliment: say something positive about what they shared, ideally linking it to a good character trait (hardworking, dedicated, brave).
3. Ask: pose another question that moves the conversation toward the problem you solve. A fitness coach might steer toward whether they find time to train; a cleaning service toward whether anyone helps with the house.
Repeat for a normal amount of back-and-forth (he suggests roughly three or four exchanges by message, a few minutes in person) before making an offer.

## When to reach for it
- Warm outreach replies, after a personalised check-in.
- Cold outreach once someone responds.
- Lead nurture texting before an appointment: keep the thread alive with acknowledge-compliment-ask, and use the ask to qualify the lead or pull the appointment earlier (Lead Nurture p. 45).
- Any time you are nervous about "what to say next".

## Mistakes he warns about
- Being weird or jumping to the ask. Pay your social dues first; you are checking in and giving value, not selling.
- Expecting to be smooth immediately. He is explicit that it takes practice.

## The math
None given. The mechanism is likeability and trust: people enjoy talking about themselves and being complimented, and they trust people who make them feel good.
