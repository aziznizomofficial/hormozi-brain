# Feature Downsell
slug: feature-downsell · category: sales · aliases: lock-and-key downsell, how-about-now close, remove the guarantee, The Minimum package, never negotiate price

## What it is
Cut the price by changing **what the customer gets**, never by selling the same thing for less. Remove quantity, quality or an entire component, drop the price, and ask whether this works for them. Customers weigh the money saved against the value lost, so a well-chosen cut often makes them see the full offer's worth and buy it after all. A high-ticket seller who offered to drop his money-back guarantee for less tripled his close rate, from 25% to 75%.

## How to apply it
1. Know your adjustable features: **quantity** (fewer sessions, a shorter supply), **quality** (response time, hours, live vs recorded, 1:1 vs group, senior vs junior staff, in-person vs remote, personalization, guarantee terms), **whole features**, and **done-for-you → done-with-you → do-it-yourself** products that solve the same problem.
2. First downsell: remove something valuable and lower the price only a little. The aim is for them to reconsider the original.
3. Keep removing features and lowering the price until they buy. Something beats nothing.
4. After two refusals, ask how much they want it on a 1–10 scale. At 8+, switch to payment-plan downsells. At 7 or below, ask what a 10 would look like and rebuild the package. Alternating the two approaches makes you very hard to refuse.
5. Close each change by checking whether it is a deal, or fair enough.
6. Name the packages: an aspirational top tier and a bottom tier called The Minimum. Confirming they want nothing beyond the minimum gets a no that means yes.
7. Refused all done-for-you? Invite them to a free orientation and sell DIY there.

## When to reach for it
Price objections, high-ticket offers, customers paying for features they don't use (downsell them before they cancel; these become some of your most valuable customers), and saving cancellations.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Cutting the price on the identical package. Match the fix to the objection: a buyer who can't pay the full amount today gets split payments; a buyer who wants a lower total gets a smaller package with parts removed.
- Being pushy. Stay a helpful guide who is finding their best deal.
- Not standardizing. Learn which combinations sell, then script them.

## The math
Removing things they dislike with a big price drop means more people take the downsell. Removing things they love with a small drop means more take the original (p. 132). Before: 25 of 100 bought. After: 35 bought full price and 40 took the downsell (p. 132). Bartering: $100 off in exchange for reviews, a video testimonial, progress posts and two introductions (p. 137).
