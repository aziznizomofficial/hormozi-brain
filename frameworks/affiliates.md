# Affiliate Army (six steps)
slug: affiliates · category: leads · aliases: affiliates and partners, affiliate program, super-affiliates, who's got my leads

## What it is
The fourth lead getter and, per Hormozi, the one that has made him the most money. An affiliate is an independent business with its own audience that promotes your thing in exchange for money, free stuff, or both. Unlike customer referrers, they run their own advertising. Your offer to them isn't the product itself; it's a fast, simple way to earn commissions. His ALAN and Prestige Labs together did over $75M from 5,000+ affiliates. Prestige Labs sold only $150k in its first three weeks against $3M of inventory, then jumped to about $450k in week four and roughly $380k–$450k a week after that, with no ads or sales team.

## How to apply it
1. **Find ideal affiliates:** businesses with warm audiences of your customers. Ask what your best customers buy, where they go, what they do for fun, and (for consumer products) who employs them. Sort into categories: software, products, equipment, services, groups, events. Businesses showing up in several categories are prime. Stuck? Ask customers.
2. **Make them an offer** using callout, value, CTA. Call out the owner, their customers, the results they promise, what they sell, or ask your own customers who owns such a business. Value pitch: more money and leads from existing customers, high odds since their people already want it, nothing to build, deliver or support, start selling tomorrow.
3. **Qualify them by making them invest.** Make them a customer (buy and use the product to keep status) and/or an expert (paid certification that covers onboarding and ad costs). Charge 10–20% of what the average active affiliate earns in year one. Too few sign-ups: lower the bar. Too few follow through: raise it.
4. **Decide pay** (see [[affiliate-payout-tiers]]).
5. **Get them advertising** with a launch (see [[whisper-tease-shout-launch]]).
6. **Keep them advertising.**

## When to reach for it
Other businesses already own the audience you want; you can train and pay partners.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Letting affiliates sell what they won't buy themselves.
- Skipping investment; people who pay pay attention.

## The math
- Ten $10k customers a month = $1.2M/year, flat. Ten affiliates a month who each bring one $10k customer monthly = $7.8M in year one, still climbing.
- ALAN: one super-affiliate added 10 agencies/month, bringing ~50 local businesses and ~2,500 leads at ~$5 each = $12,500, then $25k, $37.5k as layers stacked; a few super-affiliates reached $1.7M/month in six months.
- Certification: average affiliate earns $40k/yr, charge $4k–$8k.
