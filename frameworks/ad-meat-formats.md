# Five Ad Meat Formats
slug: ad-meat-formats · category: marketing · aliases: ad body, ad creative types, demonstration/testimonial/education/story/faceless ads

## What it is
The "meat" is the body of an ad. It delivers on the hook and teaches the viewer about the offer, product, solution or problem at their awareness level. Looking back over his ads, Hormozi found five formats did the work across services, education, physical products, local businesses and software. He does not call them the only formats, only the ones that proved themselves. They can be mixed.

## How to apply it
Record 3–5 meats per weekly session, picking from:
1. **Demonstration:** show it being used or reacted to. Unboxings, comparisons, before-and-afters, high-production "hero" ads, showing the lead magnet while explaining it, or showing the result of a service (for example, a packed gym).
2. **Testimonial:** UGC, founder or customer direct to camera, podcast-style clips, polished or raw phone testimonials, walk-and-talk rants, group "parade of proof" montages, lifecycle ads, man-on-the-street interviews, influencer collaborations.
3. **Education:** explainers, how-tos, listicles, whiteboard breakdowns, repurposed high-performing organic content.
4. **Story:** narratives, lifestyle, emotional or comedic pieces, skits, documentary style, warnings and opportunities, brand manifestos, problem-solution arcs.
5. **Faceless:** screenshots of customer messages, text-only ads, slideshows, animation, cartoons, visual-effect ads.

When stuck on what to make, go down this list.

## When to reach for it
- Hooks are strong but the ad body feels repetitive.
- The founder does not want to be on camera. Testimonial and faceless formats remove that dependency.
- You need variety for the assembly process.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Over-investing in bodies. The meat gets about 20% of prep time and is rotated less often, because fewer people watch far enough to use it up.
- Assuming what you personally dislike will not work. He hated his one lifestyle/wealth ad, but it performed.

## The math
None beyond volume: 3–5 meats per session feed the 50 × 3–5 × 1–3 assembly.
