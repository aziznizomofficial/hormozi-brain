# Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate (the 3Ds of training)
slug: document-demonstrate-duplicate · category: scaling · aliases: 3Ds, checklist training, train employees to get leads

## What it is
Hormozi's training model for turning a new hire into a lead getter. Ready-made lead getters exist but cost more; early on you usually train. The goal: a checklist clear enough that a stranger could get your results if you disappeared tomorrow.

## How to apply it
1. **Document.** Write down each step exactly as you do the task, or have someone watch and record you, ideally across several sessions. Then do your next work block following only the checklist. If that alone produces an A+ result, you have draft one.
2. **Demonstrate.** Do it in front of the trainee, walking the checklist step by step. Wherever they stop or slow you, fix the checklist. That is draft two.
3. **Duplicate.** They do it while you watch. If the result differs, the checklist is wrong; fix it and repeat until they nail it.

Coaching rules while training:
- Confusion means the instructions are confusing. A step that needs explaining is too complicated or is really several steps.
- Competence differs from performance: knowing what to do and being fast at it are separate. Slow, then smooth, then fast; the fix is reps, not new instructions.
- Grade whether they followed directions more than whether they got the result. If they followed exactly and still got it wrong, the directions are wrong; praise them anyway and fix the checklist on the spot.
- Praise each correct step. When they slip, pause and let them retry instead of taking over. No penalties during training.
- One step and one piece of feedback at a time.
- When performance drops, retrain: someone has usually dropped a step without realising it mattered, then reward doing that step.

## When to reach for it
Hiring lead-getting staff (setters, outreach reps, content assistants), or handing off any repeatable task.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Forcing a weak checklist to work; it falls apart when someone else takes over training.
- Needing long explanations or multiple demos before it clicks; that signals the checklist needs work.

## The math
No numbers in this section; the test is binary: can a stranger match your output from the checklist alone?
