# Employees as Lead Getters (internal core four)
slug: employees-as-lead-getters · category: scaling · aliases: lead-getting employees, recruiting with the core four, payroll per engaged lead, replace yourself

## What it is
The second lead getter: people on your payroll trained to run the core four for you. Hormozi calls this the book's most valuable chapter. Opening story: cold-outreach sales missed target two quarters running; the real bottleneck was one-on-one screening interviews (about forty to land one frontline rep). Switching to group interviews let hiring outpace churn, and cold outreach sales doubled within the quarter.

## How to apply it
- **Swap doing for managing.** Trade forty hours of doing for four of managing, repeatedly; then hire a manager so hundreds of hours of lead getting cost you a few hours a week.
- **Recruit with the same core four:** warm outreach = ask your network; cold outreach = recruiting; content = job posts; ads = promoted job posts; customer referrals = employee referrals; affiliates = associations, guilds, listservs; agencies = staffing firms.
- **Train with the 3Ds** (see [[document-demonstrate-duplicate]]).
- **Diagnose before firing anyone.** Ask: do my engaged leads have the problem I solve and the money to pay? No = advertising problem. Yes but too few = advertising problem. Yes but not buying = sales problem.
- For frontline roles, hire and train anyone willing; get picky only for costly senior hires. Treat the winners well.

## When to reach for it
Your own hours cap lead flow; you want the business to run without you.

## Mistakes he warns about
- "Nobody can do it like me." He replaced it with: if you want it done right, get someone doing it full time; if I can do it, someone can do it better; everyone is replaceable, especially me.
- Competing with your own staff; their weakness usually traces back to how you hired and trained them.
- Firing sales staff for an ad problem, or vice versa.

## The math
- Payroll / engaged leads = cost per lead. $100k / 1,000 = $100; 1 in 10 buys = $1,000 CAC; $4,000 LTGP = 4:1.
- Acquisition.com: roughly 30,000 engaged leads a month from a ~$100k/month content team, about $3.33 per lead, no ads or outreach.
- Why it makes you wealthy: a $2M-profit business that needs you is a job; the same profit running without you could sell for $10M+. Quote: "You become wealthy from what you own." (p. 289)
