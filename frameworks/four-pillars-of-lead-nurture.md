# Four Pillars of Lead Nurture (Show-Rate System)
slug: four-pillars-of-lead-nurture · category: leads · aliases: lead nurture, show rates, schedule rate, throughput, ALAN findings, availability-speed-personalization-volume

## What it is
Lead nurture covers the stretch between someone raising their hand and someone buying. The goal is to get more of them to schedule and actually show up within about 30 days. The framework came out of Hormozi's appointment software, ALAN. A data scientist analysed roughly a million rows of appointment data and found four behaviours linked to higher show-ups: more open slots, booking fewer days out, more follow-ups, and leads replying to those follow-ups. These became four pillars, plus a fifth requirement: actually executing.

## How to apply it
1. **Measure three numbers:** schedule rate (engaged leads who book), show rate (booked leads who attend) and throughput (engaged leads who end up attending).
2. **Availability:** open more days, hours and start times, and offer several ways to book.
3. **Speed:** contact new leads within minutes, book within about 72 hours, pull appointments forward, and answer messages fast.
4. **Personalization:** talk on their preferred channel, qualify and route leads, segment messages, give reasons to show up, and send proof that matches their situation.
5. **Volume:** keep reaching out many times over the first week, send reminders, and book the next meeting from inside the current one.
6. **Execute:** build a culture that rewards doing this dull work and rank reps on it.

## When to reach for it
- Plenty of leads but a thin calendar, or a full calendar with lots of no-shows.
- Any appointment-based sale: calls, consults, demos, in-person visits.
- Before spending more on ads. Lifting show rate is often cheaper than buying more leads.

## Mistakes he warns about
- Believing show rates are out of your control, or not worth attention.
- Not counting the sales you missed as money lost.
- Looking for one silver bullet. It is many small improvements, which he calls "golden BBs."

## The math
Worked example: 100 leads × 50% schedule rate = 50 booked; × 50% show rate = 25 shows, so throughput is 25%. He also argues that a 20–40% revenue lift at 20% margins can mean 2–3x the profit in the first year.
